<p>Short answer: usually yes. Most Tesla service centers let you drop your car off before your scheduled appointment window, including before the center opens for the day. But there&#39;s an important distinction worth understanding first: dropping off early is not the same thing as getting an <em>earlier appointment</em>. Here&#39;s how each one actually works.</p>
<h2>How early drop-off works</h2>
<p>Many Tesla service centers support after-hours or early drop-off, so you don&#39;t have to time your arrival to the exact appointment slot. The general pattern:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Check your appointment confirmation in the Tesla app.</strong> When your appointment is booked, the app (or a follow-up message) usually includes center-specific drop-off instructions — whether early drop-off is supported there, and where to park.</li>
<li><strong>Park in the designated drop-off area.</strong> Centers that support this typically have marked stalls for early or overnight drop-offs, separate from customer parking.</li>
<li><strong>Confirm the drop-off in the app.</strong> Most centers ask you to mark the vehicle as dropped off (or check in) through the app so the service team knows the car has arrived and where the key is.</li>
<li><strong>Arrange your own ride.</strong> Since you&#39;re often dropping off outside business hours, you&#39;ll need a ride home, a rideshare, or someone to pick you up — the center won&#39;t be staffed to shuttle you at that hour.</li>
</ol>
<p>Not every service center offers this, and the exact process (marked stalls, key drop box, app-based check-in) varies by location. If you don&#39;t see drop-off instructions in your appointment details, call or message the center directly to ask what they support.</p>
<h2>Does dropping off early get your car serviced sooner?</h2>
<p>Generally, no — and this is the part that trips people up. Early drop-off is a convenience for <em>your</em> schedule, not a way to jump the service queue. Technicians typically work through appointments in the order they&#39;re scheduled for the day, not in the order cars physically arrive. Dropping your car off the night before your 2 p.m. slot usually just means it sits and waits until its turn, the same as if you&#39;d driven it in at 2 p.m.</p>
<p>If what you&#39;re actually after is getting your car looked at sooner than your current appointment date, early drop-off doesn&#39;t solve that. What solves it is landing an earlier <em>appointment slot</em> in the first place, which only happens when another customer cancels or reschedules and frees up a sooner date. That&#39;s a different problem, covered in our guide on <a href="/guides/how-to-get-an-earlier-tesla-service-appointment/">how to get an earlier Tesla service appointment</a>.</p>
<h2>If you&#39;re trying to get seen sooner, not just drop off sooner</h2>
<p>Since early drop-off won&#39;t move your appointment date up, here&#39;s what actually can:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Check the Tesla app for cancellations.</strong> Open your appointment and tap into the reschedule screen periodically — earlier dates appear when other customers cancel or reschedule, and they&#39;re first-come, first-served. See our <a href="/guides/tesla-service-cancellation-slots/">guide to how cancellation slots open up</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Watch more than one nearby center.</strong> A center a bit farther away may have an earlier opening than your usual one. Boston-area owners, for example, have more than one center to compare — see our <a href="/boston-tesla-service/">Boston Tesla service page</a>.</li>

<h2>FAQ</h2>
<p><strong>Can I drop off my Tesla early for service?</strong>
Usually, yes. Most service centers support dropping your car off before your scheduled time, including after-hours in many cases, but the exact process (marked stalls, key drop box, app check-in) varies by location. Check your appointment confirmation in the Tesla app for that center&#39;s specific instructions.</p>
<p><strong>Does dropping off my Tesla early mean it gets serviced sooner?</strong>
No. Centers generally work through appointments in scheduled order, not by arrival time. Dropping off early is about convenience for you, not a way to get seen ahead of your booked slot.</p>
<p><strong>Can I drop my Tesla off the night before my appointment?</strong>
At many centers, yes, if they support after-hours drop-off. You&#39;ll typically park in a designated area and check in through the app; you&#39;ll need your own way home since the center likely won&#39;t be staffed overnight.</p>
