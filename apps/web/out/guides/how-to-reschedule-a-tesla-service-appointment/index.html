How to Reschedule a Tesla Service Appointment

Need to move your Tesla service appointment? Whether your plans changed or you're hoping to get seen sooner, rescheduling is quick and happens entirely in the Tesla app. This guide walks through the exact steps, explains the timing rules, and shows you how to actually land an earlier slot when the calendar looks full.

Reschedule in the Tesla app: step by step

All Tesla service scheduling is handled through the Tesla mobile app on your phone. Here's the general flow:

  1. Open the Tesla app and make sure your vehicle is selected at the top of the screen.
  2. Tap "Service" (sometimes shown as a wrench icon or under the vehicle menu).
  3. Select your existing appointment. You'll see the date, time, and service center for the visit you already booked.
  4. Choose "Reschedule." This lets you pick a new date and time from the slots the app shows as available.
  5. Pick your new slot and confirm. The app will update your appointment and send a confirmation. Your estimate and service notes usually carry over.

That's it. There's no phone call required and no need to email the service center for a routine date change.

A couple of timing rules to know

  • You can generally reschedule up to about 24 hours before your appointment. Inside that window, the app may lock changes and you'd need to contact the service center directly. Exact cutoffs can vary by location and by the type of work, so check your appointment details.
  • Cancelling vs. rescheduling: if you no longer need service at all, you can cancel from the same screen. If you just want a different day, reschedule instead so you keep your place in the system and your existing estimate.

Why you can't always move to an earlier date

Here's the catch most people hit: the Tesla app only lets you reschedule into slots that are actually open. If the next few weeks are fully booked, the earliest date the app offers might be the same one you already have, or even later.

Rescheduling to an earlier appointment isn't a setting you can toggle on. An earlier slot has to exist first. And those earlier slots do appear, just not on a predictable schedule. They open up when other customers cancel or reschedule their own appointments, which frees up a time that was previously taken.

The problem is that a freed-up slot can get claimed within minutes. Unless you happen to open the app at the right moment, you'll usually miss it.

FAQ

Can I reschedule my Tesla service appointment more than once? Yes. There's no set limit on how many times you can change the date, as long as you make each change before the roughly 24-hour cutoff and there's an available slot to move into.

Will rescheduling change my repair estimate or lose my place? No. Rescheduling keeps your existing appointment and its notes and estimate intact, it just changes the date and time. This is different from cancelling, which removes the appointment entirely.

How do I move my appointment to an earlier date if nothing earlier is showing? You can't force it, an earlier slot has to open up first, usually from another customer's cancellation. Check the app frequently, or let SlotWatch email you the instant an earlier opening appears at your service center.
