<p>All Tesla service scheduling is handled through the Tesla mobile app on your phone. Here&#39;s the general flow:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Open the Tesla app</strong> and make sure your vehicle is selected at the top of the screen.</li>
<li><strong>Tap &quot;Service&quot;</strong> (sometimes shown as a wrench icon or under the vehicle menu).</li>
<li><strong>Select your existing appointment.</strong> You&#39;ll see the date, time, and service center for the visit you already booked.</li>
<li><strong>Choose &quot;Reschedule.&quot;</strong> This lets you pick a new date and time from the slots the app shows as available.</li>
<li><strong>Pick your new slot and confirm.</strong> The app will update your appointment and send a confirmation. Your estimate and service notes usually carry over.</li>
</ol>
<p>That&#39;s it. There&#39;s no phone call required and no need to email the service center for a routine date change.</p>
<h3>A couple of timing rules to know</h3>
<ul>
<li><strong>You can generally reschedule up to about 24 hours before your appointment.</strong> Inside that window, the app may lock changes and you&#39;d need to contact the service center directly. Exact cutoffs can vary by location and by the type of work, so check your appointment details.</li>
<li><strong>Cancelling vs. rescheduling:</strong> if you no longer need service at all, you can cancel from the same screen. If you just want a different day, reschedule instead so you keep your place in the system and your existing estimate.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Why you can&#39;t always move to an earlier date</h2>
<p>Here&#39;s the catch most people hit: the Tesla app only lets you reschedule into slots that are <strong>actually open</strong>. If the next few weeks are fully booked, the earliest date the app offers might be the same one you already have, or even later.</p>
<p>Rescheduling to an earlier appointment isn&#39;t a setting you can toggle on. An earlier slot has to <em>exist</em> first. And those earlier slots do appear, just not on a predictable schedule. They open up when other customers cancel or reschedule their own appointments, which frees up a time that was previously taken.</p>
<p>The problem is that a freed-up slot can get claimed within minutes. Unless you happen to open the app at the right moment, you&#39;ll usually miss it.</p>

Tapping &quot;Reschedule&quot; on an existing appointment only moves your date and time at the <strong>same center</strong> you already booked — it doesn&#39;t offer a way to switch to a different location. If a nearby center might actually have something sooner, the app doesn&#39;t surface that; you&#39;d have to cancel your existing appointment and start a new booking there to find out.</p>

<h2>Can&#39;t find the Reschedule button? Quick fixes</h2>
<p>If the option is missing or greyed out, it&#39;s usually one of a few things:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>You&#39;re inside the cutoff.</strong> Within roughly 24 hours of the visit, the app typically locks changes. Message or call the center instead.</li>
<li><strong>The appointment type doesn&#39;t allow it.</strong> Some bookings, such as ones tied to a specific repair estimate or parts order, may need the service center to move them. Check the details on your appointment screen or message the center from the service request.</li>
<li><strong>The app is out of date or showing the wrong vehicle.</strong> Update the Tesla app, confirm the right vehicle is selected at the top, and reopen <strong>Service</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>It&#39;s a Mobile Service visit.</strong> Those run on a separate calendar from the service center, so the dates you see can differ. See <a href="/guides/tesla-mobile-service-vs-service-center/">Tesla mobile service vs. service center</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>If Reschedule works but only shows later dates, nothing is wrong. It just means no earlier slot is open right now.</p>
<h2>How to reschedule to an earlier date (not just a different one)</h2>
<p>Moving to a <em>different</em> date is a button press. Moving to an <em>earlier</em> date is a waiting game, because the slot has to be freed by someone else first. A practical routine:</p>
<ol>
<li>Open Reschedule and note the earliest date shown, so you can tell when something new appears.</li>
<li>Check at the times openings tend to appear: first thing in the morning and in the evening, when people adjust plans. Slots can be claimed within minutes, so a quick look beats a long browse.</li>
<li>If an earlier slot shows up, pick it and confirm right away. Don&#39;t cancel your current appointment first. Reschedule directly so you never end up with no booking.</li>

<h2>FAQ</h2>
<p><strong>How do I reschedule a Tesla service appointment for an earlier date?</strong>
Open the Tesla app, tap <strong>Service</strong>, select your appointment, tap <strong>Reschedule</strong>, and choose an earlier slot if one is listed. If none is listed, no earlier slot is open at that center right now; openings come from other customers&#39; cancellations. Check back often, or have <a href="/start/">SlotWatch</a> email you the moment one appears.</p>
<p><strong>Why can&#39;t I reschedule my Tesla service appointment in the app?</strong>
Most often you&#39;re inside the roughly 24-hour cutoff, the appointment type needs the center to change it, or the app needs an update. Contact the service center from the service request in the app if the button is missing.</p>
<p><strong>Can I reschedule my Tesla service appointment more than once?</strong>
Yes. There&#39;s no set limit on how many times you can change the date, as long as you make each change before the roughly 24-hour cutoff and there&#39;s an available slot to move into.</p>
<p><strong>Will rescheduling change my repair estimate or lose my place?</strong>
No. Rescheduling keeps your existing appointment and its notes and estimate intact, it just changes the date and time. This is different from cancelling, which removes the appointment entirely.</p>
<p><strong>How do I move my appointment to an earlier date if nothing earlier is showing?</strong>
You can&#39;t force it, an earlier slot has to open up first, usually from another customer&#39;s cancellation. Check the app frequently, or let <a href="/start/">SlotWatch</a> email you the instant an earlier opening appears at your service center.</p>
<p><strong>Can I reschedule my Tesla appointment to a different service center?</strong>
Not from the standard reschedule screen — that only changes the date and time at your current center. To move to a different one, you&#39;d cancel the existing appointment and book fresh there. <a href="/start/">SlotWatch</a> watches up to 3 centers at once, so you can see whether another location has an earlier opening before you cancel anything.</p>
<p><strong>Can I reschedule a Tesla service appointment within 24 hours?</strong>
Usually not from the app. Inside roughly 24 hours of your visit the Reschedule option is typically locked, and the cutoff can vary by center and type of work. Your options are to message or call the service center directly and ask, or to keep the original time. Don&#39;t cancel first to &quot;reset&quot; things; you could lose your spot and end up with a much later date.</p>
<p><strong>Why does the Tesla app say there are no service appointments available?</strong>
It means every slot the app is allowed to offer at that center is currently taken, often for several weeks out in busy metros. Openings return when other customers cancel or reschedule, so the screen can look different an hour later. Checking another nearby center, or letting <a href="/start/">SlotWatch</a> watch up to 3 centers for you, is the practical way to catch one. Waits vary a lot by city; see <a href="/guides/tesla-service-wait-times/">Tesla service wait times</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Can I reschedule my Tesla service appointment on a computer or by phone?</strong>
Scheduling is built around the Tesla mobile app, so that&#39;s the reliable route. If you can&#39;t use the app, call or message your service center and ask them to move the appointment. They can only offer what the calendar currently has open, so an earlier date still depends on a cancellation.</p>
<p><strong>Can I drop my Tesla off early instead of rescheduling to an earlier appointment?</strong>
Dropping off early and rescheduling to an earlier date are two different things. Many centers let you leave the car before your slot, but it still waits for its scheduled turn, so your service date doesn&#39;t change. Only rescheduling into a freed-up earlier slot moves the date. See <a href="/guides/can-i-drop-off-my-tesla-early-for-service/">can I drop off my Tesla early for service?</a> for how early drop-off works and which centers support it.</p>
<p><strong>Why does the Tesla app only show later dates when I try to reschedule?</strong>
Because the reschedule screen lists only slots that are open right now, and at a busy center the earliest open slot is often later than the one you already hold. Your current date is already the best the calendar had, so nothing is broken. A sooner date appears only when another customer cancels or moves their visit, and those openings can be taken within minutes. <p><strong>Is there a fee to reschedule a Tesla service appointment?</strong>
Tesla doesn&#39;t publicly list a fee for moving a routine service appointment, and none is shown in the app&#39;s reschedule flow. If you&#39;re dropping a paid or deposit-based service, check the terms on your appointment confirmation.</p>
<p><strong>How do I cancel a Tesla service appointment?</strong>
Open the Tesla app, tap <strong>Service</strong>, select the appointment and look for the cancel option next to Reschedule. If you only want a different date, reschedule instead of cancelling: cancelling first can leave you with no booking and a longer wait for the next opening. If you&#39;re hunting for a sooner slot, keep your current appointment as a safety net until you&#39;ve confirmed the new one.</p>
