How SlotWatch works

We watch your Tesla service center around the clock and email you the moment an earlier appointment opens up.

Tesla service waits can stretch weeks, sometimes close to two months. But slots open all the time when other owners cancel or reschedule. The trick is catching one before someone else does. That is what SlotWatch is for.

How it works

  1. Tell us your center and email. Pick up to 3 US Tesla service centers to watch and give us the email address where you want alerts.
  2. We check every ~15 minutes. Our watcher keeps an eye on your centers day and night, so you do not have to keep refreshing the Tesla app.
  3. We email you the moment an earlier slot opens. When an opening appears sooner than your current appointment, you get an email right away with the details.
  4. You reschedule in the Tesla app in seconds. Open the app, grab the slot, and you are done. The appointment always stays in your hands.

Why no Tesla login?

We never ask for your Tesla username, password, or account access, and we never store any Tesla credentials. SlotWatch only needs your email and the service centers you want to watch. Because we stay completely outside your Tesla account, you keep full control: rescheduling happens in the official Tesla app, by you. Nothing to hand over, nothing to worry about.

What we can and can't do

What we can do:

  • Watch up to 3 US Tesla service centers for you.
  • Check roughly every 15 minutes so you find out fast.
  • Surface earlier openings that appear when other people cancel or reschedule.
  • Email you the details so you can act quickly.

What we can't do:

  • We can't create or book appointments for you. Rescheduling always happens in the Tesla app.
  • We can't guarantee that an earlier slot will appear. If nobody cancels, there is nothing to find, and that is out of our hands.
  • We are honest about this: SlotWatch improves your odds of catching a cancellation, but it can't promise one.

Why I built this

Why I built this

I drive a Tesla, and last time I needed service the app quoted me a wait that was weeks out. Annoying, but I figured I was stuck with it.

Then a friend mentioned that earlier slots open up all the time when other people cancel. So I started doing the thing you probably know too well: opening the app, refreshing, closing it, opening it again a few hours later. I caught one earlier date by luck, and it made me wonder how many I'd missed just by not looking at the right moment.

That felt like something a computer should do instead of me. So I built SlotWatch to keep an eye on the service centers I care about and email me the second an earlier slot shows up.

That's it. I hope it saves you some refreshing too.

No. SlotWatch never asks for or stores your Tesla credentials, and no Tesla login is required to use our service. We monitor publicly available service center availability, and you reschedule any earlier slot yourself directly in the Tesla app.

We check your watched service centers roughly every 15 minutes, so you'll get an email alert within about 15 minutes of an earlier slot appearing. Alerts are email only (no SMS), so keep an eye on your inbox and act quickly, since popular openings can get grabbed fast.

Which service centers can you watch?

You can watch real US Tesla service centers that we monitor. Just pick the locations you want to keep an eye on, and we'll track them for earlier openings that free up from cancellations.

You can watch up to 3 US Tesla service centers at the same time on a single watch. This lets you cover multiple nearby locations to improve your chances of catching an earlier opening.
