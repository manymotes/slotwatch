<p><strong>We watch your Tesla service center around the clock and email you the moment an earlier appointment opens up.</strong></p>
<p>Tesla service waits can stretch weeks, sometimes close to two months. But slots open all the time when other owners cancel or reschedule. The trick is catching one before someone else does. That is what SlotWatch is for.</p>
<hr>
<h2>How it works</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Tell us your center, email, and current appointment date.</strong> Pick up to 3 US Tesla service centers to watch, give us the email address where you want alerts, and the date of the appointment you already have. Starting is free — no card required.</li>
<li><strong>We check every ~5 minutes.</strong> Our watcher keeps an eye on your centers day and night, so you do not have to keep refreshing the Tesla app.</li>
<li><strong>We email you the moment an earlier slot opens.</strong> Every alert arrives in full — the center and the exact date and time — so you can reschedule in the Tesla app right away. Afterward we&#39;ll ask whether you got it; if you did, you can chip in what it was worth. If not, you pay nothing.</li>
<li><strong>You reschedule in the Tesla app in seconds.</strong> Open the app, grab the slot, and you are done. The appointment always stays in your hands.</li>
</ol>
<hr>
<h2>Why no Tesla login?</h2>
<p>We never ask for your Tesla username, password, or account access, and we never store any Tesla credentials. SlotWatch only needs your email and the service centers you want to watch. Because we stay completely outside your Tesla account, you keep full control: rescheduling happens in the official Tesla app, by you. Nothing to hand over, nothing to worry about.</p>
<hr>
<h2>What we can and can&#39;t do</h2>
<p><strong>What we can do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Watch up to 3 US Tesla service centers for you.</li>
<li>Check roughly every 5 minutes so you find out fast.</li>
<li>Surface earlier openings that appear when other people cancel or reschedule.</li>
<li>Email you the details so you can act quickly.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>What we can&#39;t do:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>We can&#39;t create or book appointments for you. Rescheduling always happens in the Tesla app.</li>
<li>We can&#39;t guarantee that an earlier slot will appear. If nobody cancels, there is nothing to find, and that is out of our hands.</li>
<li>We are honest about this: SlotWatch improves your odds of catching a cancellation, but it can&#39;t promise one.</li>
</ul>
<hr>
<h2>Ready to stop refreshing the app?</h2>
<p><strong>Start watching your service center today — free, no card, every alert in full. If it works, pay what it was worth.</strong></p>
