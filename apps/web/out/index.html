Questions

Do I need to connect my Tesla account?
No. You never connect your Tesla account, and we never ask for your login or password. You just tell us which service center to watch and where to email you. When an earlier opening appears, you reschedule it yourself in the Tesla app.
What does it cost?
Nothing up front: no card, no fee. Every alert arrives in full with the center, the exact date and time. If an alert gets you an earlier appointment, we ask afterward whether you’d like to chip in what it was worth to you (suggested $19). If it didn’t help, you pay nothing. Keeping a watch running past your appointment date is an optional $6.99/mo.
Which service centers does SlotWatch watch?
Any Tesla Service Center in the US. You pick by city or ZIP when you sign up, and you can watch up to three at once — a second center nearby roughly doubles your odds.
How quickly will I be alerted?
We check your centers about every 5 minutes. A slot on an earlier day than anything we’ve told you about emails you right away. Extra times on days you already know about are batched into at most one email every few hours, so you’re never flooded.
What counts as an earlier slot?
Any opening at one of your watched centers on an earlier day than the appointment date you gave us. A same-day slot at an earlier time doesn’t count.
What if the slot is gone by the time I check?
Cancellations get grabbed fast, so open the Tesla app the moment an alert lands. If it was already taken you’ve lost nothing, and we keep watching.
What happens when my appointment date arrives?
Your watch ends automatically. You can start a new one for a future appointment, or opt into $6.99/mo to keep watching continuously.
