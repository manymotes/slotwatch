Tesla Service Pittsburgh-Wexford (14010 Perry Hwy, 15090) is in the North Hills along Route 19, closer to the North Hills, Cranberry, and Butler County. Both carry “Pittsburgh” in their names, so check the street address on your appointment confirmation if you’re not sure which one you booked. Frequently asked — Pittsburgh

Which Tesla service centers does SlotWatch watch in Pittsburgh?

SlotWatch can watch any of the 2 Tesla Service Centers in the Pittsburgh area — including Pittsburgh-Presto, Pittsburgh-Wexford. You can monitor up to 3 of them at once on a single watch.

How quickly will I get the alert?

SlotWatch checks availability about every 5 minutes. When an earlier opening appears in your date range, we email you right away so you can reschedule in the Tesla app before it fills.

Do I need to connect my Tesla account?

No — you never connect your Tesla account and we never see your login. Just tell us which Pittsburgh service center to watch and where to email you. When an earlier slot opens, you reschedule it yourself in the Tesla app.

Why would an earlier appointment open up?

Tesla service in Pittsburgh is often booked weeks out, but people cancel and reschedule constantly — each time, an earlier slot briefly opens. SlotWatch catches those the moment they appear so you don't have to refresh the app.

Is there a free option?

Yes — SlotWatch itself is free: no card, no fee, watch up to 3 centers, every alert in full. If an alert gets you an earlier appointment, you can chip in what it was worth afterward (suggested $19), but that’s up to you. You never connect your Tesla login. A self-hosted version is also on GitHub, free and open source.

What counts as an earlier slot?

Any opening at one of your watched centers on an earlier day than the appointment date you gave us. We email you the center and the exact date and time, in full, free. A same-day slot at an earlier time doesn’t count.

What if I find a slot myself, or the slot is gone by the time I check?

Nothing — SlotWatch is free. Finding one on your own costs nothing, and so does an alert that didn’t work out. Cancellations do get grabbed fast, so open the Tesla app the moment an alert lands. We keep watching either way.

When do I pay?

You don’t have to. SlotWatch is free: no card, no fee, every alert in full. After an alert, we’ll ask whether you managed to book it. If you did, you can chip in whatever it was worth to you — $9, $19 or $49, or any amount. If it didn’t work, you pay nothing.
